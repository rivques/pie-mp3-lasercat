There are 4 major components that need power:
Motors - 160mA-1.5A@7.2V per https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/seeed-technology-co-ltd/114090050/10385087 - probably 600-1000ma total under load @9-12V?
Arduino - ~100mA@5V
Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W - 200-400mA@5V per https://www.cnx-software.com/2021/12/09/raspberry-pi-zero-2-w-power-consumption/
Camera - ~100mAish per https://forums.raspberrypi.com/viewtopic.php?t=347469

The camera must be connected to the raspi, so they have a combined power draw of 300-500mA@5V.
A 9V battery cannot power the motors, per https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-motor-shield-v2-for-arduino/powering-motors, and it is strongly recommended that they be on a separate supply from everything else, or at least very isolated.
If the Arduino and raspi communicate over USB serial, they will share power unless we specifically cut VBUS.
So that leaves:

Motors: 300-1000mA@5-9V
Everything else: 400-600mA@5V

Available power sources:
12V@>1.5A PIE-supplied wallwart. Must be plugged into wall.
11.1V@55A 3S LiPo. Needs charged, must be wary of short, fuse ideal.
6-9V@2ishA 4-6xAA battery pack. Would have to acquire.
9V@300mA? 9V battery.
5V@2.4A USB power bank. Bulky. 
5V@1A Arduino onboard regulator. Needs 6.5-12V input.
5V@1.5A external LM7805 regulator. Needs 6.5-12V input.

Options:
Easiest: 12V from PIE supply to motors and arduino regulator, raspi and cam backpowered over USB. Overvolts motors.
Easy portable: 5V from USB power bank to raspi, arduino powered over raspi. 5V from USB power bank to motors w/ cut USB cable. Pros: Have everything on hand. Cons: only 0.5m/s, need to cut cable.
Faster portable: 6xAA to Arduino regulator. CHS-style 4xAA split to motors, or not. Use external regulator if more current needed. Pros: portable, high-current, recommended config. Cons: Need to aquire 6xAAs and case.

Other, worse options:
- LiPo to motors and Arduino/external regulator.
- 4xAA/LiPo/9V to motors, USB power bank to raspi.

Motor calculations:
https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/seeed-technology-co-ltd/114090050/10385087
Assume maybe 150rpm loaded speed at 5V. Wheel is ~60mm diameter. Max speed is (60*pi)mm/rev*150rev/min to m/s = ~0.5m/s At 7.2V, 0.65m/s. Extrapolating, maybe 0.8m/s@9V and 1m/s@12V ?
